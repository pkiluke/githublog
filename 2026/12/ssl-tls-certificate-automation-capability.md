## Introduction
Welcome dear readers,

Today, I will talk about digital certificates or more specifically the SSL/TLS digital certificates and their corresponding automation capability in ABB. 

Why should a company care about certificate automation? Because the most immediate reason for certificate automation are drastic certificate lifetime changes of public SSL/TLS certificates (i.e., scheduled in 2026, 2027, 2029; read more below). The other reasons are the growth of machine identities for IoT/Edge and containerized services, closing the “expired certificate” gap, avoiding the cost of failure resulting from regulatory fines and cryptographic agility.

For the more technical readers, please keep in mind that I am simplifying a lot of the contents so that the article is understandable to a broader audience.

Enjoy the reading!
